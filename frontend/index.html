Important Info

What to Know

Everything you need for a great session

👟

What to Wear

  • Trainers or astro football boots only
  • NO studs or blades allowed
  • Shin pads MUST be worn — no exceptions
  • Comfortable sportswear
💧

What to Bring

  • Water bottle — stay hydrated!
  • Any required medications (e.g. inhalers)
  • £1 cash for the session
  • Lots of energy and a smile!
📸

Photo Policy

  • Photographs will be taken during sessions
  • Used to promote the club and sessions
  • Notify staff if you do NOT want your child photographed
  • You can opt out during registration
🅿️

Getting There

  • Bakersfield, NG3 7EB
